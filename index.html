About Mrudul Kulkarni

I am a Computer Engineering student at MES Wadia College of Engineering, Pune, with a passion for low-level systems, developer tooling, and creative computing.

Most of my time is spent experimenting with C, Java, and Python, building everything from terminal text engines to OpenStreetMap geodata utilities, while diving into modern web frontend architecture with React.

Beyond code, I am fascinated by philosophy, linguistics, and speculative fiction — particularly the idea of treating data and networks not merely as static infrastructure, but as living physical processes in motion.
